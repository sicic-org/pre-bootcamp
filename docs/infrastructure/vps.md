# VPS: Your Sovereign Digital Territory

Before we touch any code, we must answer the most common question: **"Why do I need a VPS (Virtual Private Server)? Why can't I just use ChatGPT or Claude on my browser?"**

### The "Why": Sovereignty & Asynchrony

Using a web browser AI is like being a day laborer. You log in, you ask a question, you get an answer, and when you close the tab, the AI "dies." It forgets your context, and it stops working.

A **VPS** is your sovereign territory in the cloud. It is a computer that you rent, and it runs 24/7. 
* **Sovereignty**: You own the data. You own the logs. Nobody can suddenly change the UI or ban your account and lock you out of your workflow.
* **Asynchrony (The Real Leverage)**: A VPS allows your AI to work while you sleep. You can tell your Agent at 11 PM: "Research these 50 competitors," close your laptop, and wake up to a finished report. 

You are not renting an app; you are buying land to build a digital factory. We use **Linode** because it provides root access (total control) and is highly reliable for running AI workloads.
